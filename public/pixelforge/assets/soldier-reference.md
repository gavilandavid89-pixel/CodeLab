# Soldier reference-guided animation

Source reference: generated pixel-art infantry soldier used as the anatomy/style guide for this showcase.

Visual invariants carried into the animation:
- full-body adult infantry silhouette
- helmet, face proportions, tactical vest and pouches
- camouflage/tan armor palette with dark outlines and warm highlights
- knee protection, boots and rifle held diagonally across the torso
- compact pixel-art edges and high-contrast shading
- dark neutral background and grounded contact shadow

Animation study:
- 24 frames
- 16 FPS
- walk cycle through frame 15
- controlled transition into crouch through frame 23
- same anatomy proportions, equipment placement and palette across the timeline

The source artwork remains the visual reference; the runtime animation is a CodeLab-native raster reconstruction rather than a second unrelated character design.
